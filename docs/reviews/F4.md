Created: 2026-10-18 17:12 -05:00 · Edited: 2026-10-18 17:12 -05:00

**Reviewed:** PR #7 (`f3e9f53..ece5fc5`), by the adversarial reviewer in a fresh context, 2026-10-18, with the dock unplugged and the Mac on battery. **The PR had already been merged when the review started**, so the verdict is what must land as a fix: one major, landed as the retrofit **F4b** (PR #9), plus seven minors.

## What the reviewer ran

`swift build`, `swift test` (45, 0 failures), mutations M5 and M12 (caught), the bare binary for two minutes with its menu read through System Events, and a **replay harness** compiled against the built `DockCore` that fed the eighteen real `usb.detached` lines of the one recorded dock pull (17:30:29Z) through the real tracker, membership rule, coalescer and query with the app's decision lines copied verbatim. That replay is what found the major.

## Attacked and held

The 500-ring's `removeFirst` at 0.6 µs; the tail's torn-line heuristic unreachable within the last 500 lines; seeding on the main thread in milliseconds; both coalescer orderings schedule exactly one flush and never double-post; `asleep` gating matches FR6's wording; the `isAvailable` guard precedes every notification-center call; 12 MB; no AppKit in DockCore; no timer in the diff.
