## Findings and what was done

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |

The owner ratifies D18. |

## Attacked and held

Message constants match `IOMessage.h`; sleep is always allowed; `stop()` order is Apple's. Power-source ownership rules and `"AC Power"` correct; `lastWatts` on disconnect is the right reading of "with negotiated watts". The design's load-bearing assumption held by the daemon's own code: on a cable pull diskarbitrationd fires *disappeared* before its forced unmount, so the clean/unclean split is real. DiskArbitration replays every disk within 15 ms of scheduling; Time Machine snapshot mounts have no BSD name and are dropped. The IORegistry walk leaks nothing. All public API; no timers; no AppKit in DockCore. Note for F4: a future unknown kind throws on decode, so F4's reader must skip a bad line rather than fail the file.
