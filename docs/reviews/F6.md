# Review — F6 eject dock volumes

Created: 2026-10-18 13:58 -05:00 · Edited: 2026-10-18 13:58 -05:00

**Reviewed:** commits `47e2fde` and `cb68a03` on `F6/eject`, by the adversarial reviewer in a fresh context, 2026-10-18. **Verdict: do not merge as built.** Three majors that are one defect seen from three sides: the feature promised a reported result and a named blocker, and for the two cases the owner would actually hit, a successful eject and Time Machine holding Backups, the menu showed nothing and "another process". Six minors. All addressed in the follow-up commit; the owner's live check waits for the dock to be plugged back in.

## What the reviewer ran

`swift build`, `swift test` (31, 0 failures), mutations M7 and M9 (caught), standalone C probes against libproc and a standalone DiskArbitration probe on throwaway images (double unmount, busy, not mounted), the app four times driven through System Events (listing, a blocked eject with `tail -f` holding a file, a successful eject, a remount), `ioreg`, the SDK's `mach/error.h`, `DADissenter.h` and `libproc.h`, the diskarbitrationd log, and the green CI run. Only Down Under was ejected, and it was remounted each time.

**Environment note from the review.** At 13:23 the dock's remaining disks were ejected by Finder-style solicitations and the dock was disconnected; the Mac went to battery. Not the review's doing (DockWatch never calls `DADiskEject` and logged no `volume.eject`). Live testing ended there.

## Findings and what was done

| # | Severity | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | A successful eject was never shown: the result was keyed to a row that only exists while the volume is mounted, and a successful eject unmounts it. O14 told the owner to look for "Ejected" beside a row that could not exist. | **Fixed**: `EjectMenuModel` (DockCore, pure) keeps a just-ejected volume as a row without an action for the result's lifetime. M10 covers it. |
| 2 | major | A stale "Ejected" sat beside a volume that had been mounted again, for up to 60 s. The owner reading it and pulling the drive is the data-loss path. | **Fixed**: a `volume.mounted` for that BSD name clears the result. M11 covers it. |
| 3 | major | Root-owned blockers can never be named: `proc_listpidspath` from an unprivileged process lists only the caller's user's processes and `proc_name` returns nothing for root pids. With a backup running, "Eject All" said "In use by another process", while RR13 and O14 promised "In use by backupd". | **Recorded, worded honestly**: the empty case now reads "In use by a system process"; RR13 and O14 rewritten to what a non-root app can say; RR12's sandbox check extended to `processesUsing`. |
| 4 | minor | Non-busy refusals reached the user as hex (`0xf8da0007`); `DADissenterGetStatusString` was nil in every observed case. | **Fixed**: the DiskArbitration return codes have words ("Not mounted", "Not permitted", …). |
| 5 | minor | A click on a row whose volume vanished while the menu was open was silently dropped. | **Fixed**: reported as "No longer mounted", with a log line. |
| 6 | minor | "Eject" is an unmount; the disk stays attached and remounts with `diskutil`, unlike Finder's eject. The menu word carried Finder's meaning without a decision behind it. | **Recorded as D23 (proposed)**: unmount by design, so a remount needs no replug; a whole-disk eject is a v1.5 option. |
| 7 | minor | The code called libproc "public API"; its SDK header calls its interfaces private and subject to change, and NFR1 does not list it. | **Comment corrected; RR15 opened.** One call site, and the menu degrades to "a system process" if it ever fails. |
| 8 | minor | Every ejector test paid for a disk image; two of three needed none. ~10 s of a 12 s suite. | **Fixed**: pure tests moved to `EjectResultTests`; only `VolumeEjectorLiveTests` attaches an image. Suite now 2.4 s. |
| 9 | minor | The plan's Design was rewritten in place after the build (result type, menu shape, 10 s → 60 s) as if it had always said so; one evidence row wrongly said the subtitle could not be read back. | **Fixed**: a "Design changes after the gate" section lists the three changes; the evidence row corrected (the accessibility name of a row includes its subtitle). |

## Attacked and held

The `Unmanaged` box is always balanced because DiskArbitration always calls back once the daemon replies; `DADiskCreateFromBSDName` follows the Create rule. `unixError` matches `mach/error.h`. The controller-byte rule: `ioreg` shows exactly three XHCI controllers and every dock device under `0x02`; a second Thunderbolt dock would get its own byte. `present.values.min()` is deterministic. `proc_listpidspath` buffer sizing is safe; names are not truncated at 16 characters. `menuNeedsUpdate` rebuilds on every open; ⌘E is scoped to the open menu; the `#available(macOS 14.4)` subtitle falls back to the title on the 14.0 floor. No timers; DockCore is AppKit-free.
