Learning level 5% (D6); every task is `owner: agent`. Last feature of the MVP; added after the baseline by D16, D20 and D26.

## What the user gets

The owner's colour wheel in the menu bar instead of the connector symbols: full colour when the dock is connected, faint and grey when it is not. It sits still. While "Keep Dock Awake" is holding the Mac it turns slowly, one revolution a minute, so the hold is visible as motion. When the dock connects or disconnects it makes one quick turn. In Finder and the Dock the app shows the same ring, edge to edge, as an Icon Composer icon.

## Design

- `RingMotion` (DockCore, pure): phase, held flag, and the remaining fraction of a one-turn flourish. `isMoving` is true only while held or mid-turn; `tick()` advances one frame. The app never ticks a still ring (M13).
- `RingIcon` (DockWatch, CoreGraphics): draws the annulus with a conic hue gradient of 24 stops at a given phase, 18 pt, same inner/outer ratio as the artwork, red at the lower right. The not-connected look is the same ring desaturated and faint. No bitmap is rotated; the gradient's start angle is the phase.
- Motion driver (`StatusItemController`): a `Timer` at 12 frames a second that exists **only while `RingMotion.isMoving`**, with a 40 ms tolerance so the system can coalesce it. This is an animation clock, not a poll of any state; D29 records the NFR2 reading. At rest there is no timer at all.
- Finder icon (D26): `Resources/AppIcon.icon`, an Icon Composer document with one layer, the regenerated ring, on a near-white fill; `scripts/build-app.sh` compiles it with `actool` as HueBar does, producing `Assets.car` and a fallback `.icns`. The committed flat `.icns` from F5 goes.

## Design changes after the gate

1. The conic gradient is drawn as 72 filled sectors, not with CoreGraphics' conic gradient call: that method is not exposed to Swift on this SDK. At 18 pt the sectors are invisible; at 8× magnification faint edges show.
2. The not-connected look is the ring in the system's secondary label colour at 55 percent, so it follows light and dark menu bars.
3. After the review: the slow turn runs at one frame a second, not twelve; the timer is destroyed while the status item is not visible; a filled hub marks the held state in a still glance; Reduce Motion disables motion; no flourish on the first render; a flourish and a released hold both land the ring at home; the flourish is 1.25 s so its frames divide evenly; the build script writes a full ten-size `.icns` beside `Assets.car` for macOS 14 and 15.
