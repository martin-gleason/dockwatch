## What the user gets

Open the menu and see the last 20 events and four numbers for the past 24 hours: devices lost, volumes lost, charger drops, wakes. When a drive or the keyboard vanishes while the Mac is awake, a notification says which one.

## Design

- `EventQuery` (DockCore, pure): `recent(n:)` and `counters(window:)` over an in-memory ring of the last 500 events, seeded from the tail of the log at launch. The 24-hour window is computed from the event timestamp, not insertion order (M5).
- `TimelineMenu` (DockWatch): a submenu of `NSMenuItem`s rebuilt when the menu opens (`NSMenuDelegate.menuWillOpen`), never on a timer (NFR2). The counters line notes that sleep-time charger drops are in the system power log, not here (RR3).
- `LossCoalescer` (DockCore, pure): collects dock losses (`usb.detached` of a dock device, `volume.disappeared` of a dock volume) for two seconds after the first; if the dock device itself detached in that window the flush is one "dock disconnected" summary, otherwise one line per lone loss (D22, D27). Only while awake.
- `LossNotifier` (DockWatch): `UNUserNotificationCenter`, fed by the coalescer's flushes. Permission requested once at the installed app's first launch; a bare binary logs a note instead (RR6).
