# F5 — Installable app and launch at login

Created: 2026-10-18 10:55 -05:00 · Edited: 2026-10-18 20:55 -05:00

**Status:** built 2026-10-18; reviewed (`docs/reviews/F5.md`); the dock half of the sandbox proof done 2026-10-18 20:45 from the running bundle (A1, below); the login launch itself stays off until D30 (O17).

Order per D19: after F6, before F4. Without a bundle the app cannot post notifications or register for login. Learning level 5% (D6); every task is `owner: agent`.

## Design

- `scripts/build-app.sh`: `swift build -c release`, wrap into `build/DockWatch.app` with `Resources/Info.plist` (`LSUIElement` true, bundle id `com.martingleason.dockwatch`), **App Sandbox and hardened runtime** (D15) from `Resources/DockWatch.entitlements`, ad-hoc signed, `--install` copies to `~/Applications`. Mirrors HueBar's script.
- App icon: the owner's hue ring (D16) rendered to `Resources/AppIcon.icns` from the regenerated 1024 px source with `sips` and `iconutil`. Drawing the ring in the menu bar is F7; this is the Finder and Dock icon.
- Under the sandbox the app's home is its container, so the log lives at `~/Library/Containers/com.martingleason.dockwatch/Data/Library/Logs/DockWatch/events.jsonl`; "Open Log" reveals it (D24).
- Sandbox proof (RR12): the bundled, sandboxed app must still see the USB priming pass, take the sleep assertion, unmount a volume, and name a blocker. Each is checked from the installed bundle and recorded, pass or fail.
- `LaunchAtLogin` (DockWatch): `SMAppService.mainApp.register()` / `unregister()`, status read from `SMAppService.mainApp.status`. A menu toggle.

## Design changes after the gate

## Addendum — two things the proof run showed (2026-10-18 20:55)

1. **Priming order leaves the first devices unmarked.** Of the 20 dock devices logged at launch, the seven enumerated before the dock's own device (`External USB 3.0`, `Das Keyboard`, `USB Receiver`, two hubs, the Chroma controller, `USB2.1 Hub`) carry no `dock=true`, because membership is judged as each event arrives and the tracker had not yet seen the dock. The counters (D28) read detaches only, so nothing is miscounted, and every detach after priming carried the mark; the attach lines are wrong on disk. Filed as **A3**.
2. **The fault, live.** At 20:43:33, 34 seconds after launch and with the dock's own device still present, six devices on the dock's USB 2 chain terminated together: `USB3.1 Hub`, `USB3.0 Hub`, `USB2.1 Hub`, `USB Receiver`, `USB2.0 Hub`, `Das Keyboard`, all with `dock=true`; the counters line read `6 devices lost`. The identifying device at `0x02148000` did not terminate, so the hold policy would have held (RR14's bad case did not occur). Whether a notification was shown is the owner's to say (O15). The Mac was awake and on AC the whole time.
3. **The notification path, end to end (O15, lone half).** The launch prompt at 20:42:59 was declined, so the app's first real notification at 20:43:35 (two seconds after the six-device drop, D27 holding) was refused by Notification Center with `authorizationStatus: Denied`; nothing in the app shows that state (D32 proposed). After the owner allowed notifications in System Settings, unplugging the Apple Watch charging cable at 20:51:27 produced `usb.detached … dock=true` and `usernoted` logged `Presenting` at 20:51:30. At 20:51:40 `T1000_E_BOOT` (`0x02111000`) dropped and returned on its own two seconds later, a second lone notification at 20:51:43: the fault again, small, with the Mac awake.
